🖊️ Call for papers

Submission Requirements:

    - Manuscripts must be submitted in PDF format. Papers should follow the Overleaf ACM format published in ACM guidelines and selecting the “sigconf” sample (We recommend using \documentclass[sigconf, anonymous, review]{acmart}).
    - All submissions must not exceed 5 pages in total, including references.
    - Submitted papers will undergo double-blind review: all submissions must be properly anonymized. Non-anonymized papers will be desk-rejected without review.
    - LLM-generated text is prohibited unless part of the experimental analysis. AI tools may only be used for light editing (e.g., grammar checks).
    - Accepted papers will be published in the WSDM 2027 Companion Proceedings Volume. Authors who prefer not to have their paper included may opt out by notifying the organizers.
    - At least one author of every accepted paper must register and present the paper in person. There is no provision for remote presentations, and papers that are not presented in person will be excluded from the Companion Proceedings.

Submission Website:

Please submit your papers via Microsoft CMT (Workshop Track 17): https://cmt3.research.microsoft.com/WSDM2027/Track/17/Submission/Create
